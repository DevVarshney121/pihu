GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful details before you start a project.

How does the project process work?

Share your requirements first. After discussion, you receive a proposed scope, quotation and timeline. Work starts once the details are approved and the agreed advance is received.

Why is an advance payment required?

The agreed advance reserves project time and allows work to begin. The scope, deliverables, revisions and remaining payment schedule should be confirmed before work begins.

Can I pay using UPI or a payment link?

Yes, when a verified UPI ID or official payment link has been configured and confirmed. Contact us for payment details after approving the quotation.

Do you provide website and creative services together?

Yes. You can enquire about website development, graphics, social media content, video editing and other listed services in one project.
